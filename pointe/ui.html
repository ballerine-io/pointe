          Microphone
          
            
          Talk button
          
            
          Talk key
          
          
          
          
          In push to talk, nothing you say leaves this Mac unless you press the key or the mic button (hold it, or tap to start and
            tap again to send: this browser's choice), except
            during a meeting you started: the room is heard for its notes, and talking to Pointe still needs the hold. Caps Lock
            turns talking on and off (a toggle: a browser cannot see it held). Keys work only while this tab is focused; for any
            app, bind the shortcut below.
          
          
          
            
          System-wide shortcut
          
            
          
            
          Talk from any app, with this page in the background: press once to talk, again to stop. In the Shortcuts
              app, make a new shortcut; add the action Run Shell Script and paste the command; open its details (ⓘ) and
              Add Keyboard Shortcut, for example ⌃⌥Space. The same 90 s limit as Caps Lock applies, and the call must be up
              (the page or the iPhone app). For hold to talk from any app, a tool such as Raycast, Hammerspoon or BetterTouchTool runs
              the command with on on key down and off on key up.
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Turn taking
          
          
          
            
          Wait before answering
          
          
          
          
          How long a pause ends your sentence. Normal waits about a second after a whole sentence and three after
            a half one; Patient waits longer; Quick answers as soon as you stop. Say “don't interrupt me” or “hold on” and it stays
            quiet until you say “go ahead” or “I'm done”, or until 8 seconds of quiet.
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
          The state line says “Thinking…” from the end of your turn until the reply. The sound plays only
            when that takes long, never with the reply, and never while Pointe is muted, in whisper mode or in a meeting.
            Say “keep me posted” or “stop updates” to turn the progress updates on or off by voice.
