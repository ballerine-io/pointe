Setups: which keys Pointe needs, and for what

Written by tools/setups.py from setups.py: edit those, not this file.

Why its own keys? Pointe runs its own models next to Claude Code: the brain, the reader and the checker, the ears and the voice. Your Claude subscription (Pro or Max) runs Claude Code, but has no interface other apps can use, so Pointe's voice needs its own API key from console.anthropic.com, or one Gemini or OpenAI key. An API key is billed per use: about $1 for a busy hour of talk.

Pointe has five roles. Each setup fills all five, with the keys in its row:

  • brain: understands you and replies
  • reader: turns session output into short reports
  • checker: a safety check on each send and approval
  • ears: speech to text
  • mouth: the voice

A busy hour: 60 spoken replies, 10 session reports and the call open for the hour, by arithmetic from measured and list prices (docs/benchmarks.md, "Cost arithmetic"), not a metered bill. "Measured" is the real audio suite (tests/e2e/run_all.sh, 35 checks: 19 single-session, 12 multi-session, 4 echo), one run per setup on 2026-11-13 with only that setup's keys visible, and the per-role benchmarks in bench/. "Not measured" means no run covered it. Voices are synthetic; no setup was measured on a real human call.

No setup is best on every axis. The fastest and most accurate voice has the highest whole-setup result and the best ears and voice, but its brain (Sonnet) and the Free start's brain (Gemini) are within the margin of each other: Gemini passed more cases, Sonnet answered faster. Pick by what matters to you:

  • Best for trying Pointe at no cost: Free start (pointe setup free)
  • Best for people who already pay for OpenAI: One OpenAI key (pointe setup openai)
  • Best for the best ears with two accounts, on laptop speakers: Two keys (pointe setup two)
  • Best for daily use: Fastest and most accurate voice (pointe setup full)
  • Best for confidential work where text may leave the Mac but your voice may not: Voice stays on your Mac (pointe setup voice-local)
  • Best for work where nothing may leave the Mac, on a 32 GB Mac: Fully local (pointe setup local)

With nothing chosen, /pointe:setup asks one question, "Start with Free start?": one key, about 5 minutes, and this table only if you ask for the other setups.

Choosing and switching

pointe setup shows this table and the setup in use; pointe setup <id> switches, and from then on the daemon uses exactly that setup's vendors, whatever other keys the Keychain holds. pointe key <vendor> saves a key through a Mac window after opening the vendor's key page, checks it with one tiny live call (and asks again when the vendor refuses it), and restarts Pointe to use it; pointe key check <vendor> checks a saved key. After pointe setup <id>, restart with pointe restart. The page's Settings, Model keys, and the Mac app's do the same with buttons: Add… or Replace… for a key, Use this setup for a setup, each shown with the key as In use, Needed or Optional.
