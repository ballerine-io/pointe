Speech component benchmarks

These benchmarks chose Pointe's default vendors: one role at a time (ears, brain, mouth, gate, voice lock), on synthetic speech and invented cases. Most were run once, between 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-25, on builds up to 0.8.5, and none has been re-run on a later build. Each line gives its date, its method and where its raw rows are. The scripts are in bench/; re-run them with your own keys.

What the whole product does on a call (first word, accuracy, approvals, turn-taking, recorded people) is measured separately, on a tagged release: docs/benchmarks/README.md, and on the site.

Raw rows behind this page: bench/results/raw/ (synthetic, no corpus audio).

Ears (speech to text), 2026-11-11/12

bench/stt_extra.py; report bench/results/report.txt; raw bench/results/raw/stt-results.jsonl and stt-judged.json. 70 utterances (English, Hebrew, mixed, short yes/no) x 4 synthetic ElevenLabs voices x clean and 12 dB noise = 560 clips, streamed at real-time pace, one run per clip.

ears intent correct (model judge) word error (computed)
Soniox stt-rt-v5 95.4% 0.062
Soniox stt-rt-v6 (default) 95.9% 0.063
gemini-3.5-transcribe-live 92.0% 0.094
OpenAI gpt-4o-transcribe 90.9% 0.209
OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-transcribe 85.5% 0.203
Gemini Live, as a whole 79.6% 0.240
OpenAI gpt-live-transcribe 81.1% 0.195
ElevenLabs scribe_v2_realtime 68.9% 0.378
Whisper large-v3-turbo, local (MLX, M5 Pro, 2026-11-13) 85.7% 0.219

  • Intent is judged by a model, gemini-3.5-flash at temperature 0, blind to the vendor. It is from the same vendor as two rows it grades. Its verdicts are noisy: of 417 groups of identical reference and transcript pairs, 37 got verdicts that disagree with their group's majority, and it passed a Hebrew request transcribed into Arabic script. A difference of a few points between rows is not a ranking. Word error is computed, not judged; it is a mean of per-clip rates, so a one-word clip weighs as much as a sentence.

  • No latency column. The earlier table gave latency per vendor, but the vendors were not timed the same way: Soniox at its first end token (some clips came out negative), ElevenLabs at its last commit after a 0.5 s pause, OpenAI after a 500 ms server pause, Gemini with a fixed 0.4 s added, and clips that never gave a final were dropped from the percentiles. Those numbers do not compare, so they are not published. The time Pointe takes end to end is measured on the release benchmarks instead.

  • Pointe uses stt-rt-v6 because Soniox removes v5 on 2026-12-15, not because v6 measured better.

Brain, 2026-11-25

bench/first_word_bench.py brains with bench/brain_bench.py's 84 cases, prompts, stub tools and rubric, through the real brain.Brain; raw bench/results/raw/first_word.jsonl; .venv/bin/python bench/first_word_bench.py report recomputes it. "Right" is the rubric's code checks, not a model judge. Load median 9.5.

brain replies (84 cases x repeats) right first sentence median / p90 $ a reply
anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5 (default) 336 81.5% 2.21 / 3.82 s 0.0048
openai:gpt-6-sol 252 83.3% 1.85 / 3.47 s 0.0194
anthropic:claude-haiku-5-5 (fallback) 84 78.6% 2.23 / 3.38 s 0.0004
openai:gpt-6-luna 84 75.0% 2.42 / 3.90 s 0.0012
gemini:gemini-3.5-flash-lite 84 72.6% 1.27 / 2.23 s 0.0033
gemini:gemini-3.8-flash 61 of 84 answered 88.5% 4.69 / 9.30 s 0.0066

On the same cases, Sonnet was right where gpt-6-sol was wrong 23 times, and the reverse 28 times, of 252: no difference. The prompts were written and tuned on Sonnet, which favours it. Synthetic cases; stub tools answer at once, so these times are shorter than on a call. The earlier figures on this page (78 cases, Sonnet 91.9%) had no report or raw rows behind them and were removed.

Mouth (text to speech), 2026-11-05/06

bench/tts_bench.py run --reps 3; report bench/results/tts.md; raw bench/results/raw/tts-raw.jsonl. 24 replies (8 English, 8 Hebrew, 8 mixed) x 3, one laptop, one network path, connection already open.

eleven_v3_conversational 181 / 205 ms 0.03 / 0.05 yes 0.04
eleven_flash_v2_5 174 / 182 ms 0.04 / 1.11 no 0.04
soniox tts-rt-v2 301 / 326 ms 0.03 / 0.02 yes about 0.05
gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts 441 / 534 ms 0.03 / 0.02 yes 0.012

OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts 799 / 1815 ms 0.03 / 0.03 yes about 0.035

Word error is a round trip through a Soniox recognizer (Soniox is also a row here). The report names stt-rt-v6, but the run predates v6 and the rows do not record which model heard them. How a voice sounds to a person is not measured.

The rest, each once, synthetic

  • Gate (2026-11-06): tests/test_relevance_live.py, 24 cases, two runs per model; gemini-3.5-flash-lite failed none. The cases were written while the gate was tuned on that model. bench/results/gate.md

  • Voice lock: 19 synthetic voices (other macOS voices as impostors). At the thresholds the daemon uses, a stranger's sentence passes 1.6% of the time (16 of 1,026) and yours fails 3.5% (2 of 57); a short word after a match passes for a stranger 1.3% (9 of 684) and fails for you 5.3% (2 of 38). bench/results/speaker.md

  • Vocabulary (Soniox v5): a made-up name spelled right 25 of 64 times without hints, 60 of 64 with them. bench/results/vocab.md

  • Challenge me (2026-11-12): 15/30 to 30/30 on Sonnet, 21/30 to 30/30 on Gemini Flash, on the same six cases the brackets were designed on; no held-out cases. bench/results/challenge.md

  • Whisper (Soniox v5, made by lowering synthetic voices; no person whispered): word error 1.4% at -18 dB, 18.4% at -30 dB. bench/results/whisper.md

  • Fully local (pointe local, M5 Pro, 2026-11-13, load 17 to 154): brain gemma-4-26b-a4b-it passed 76.9% of the 78 cases of that day, median reply 3.4 s with stub tools; a plain question end to end took 11.6 s. bench/results/local.md

  • Voice stays on your Mac (pointe local voice, 2026-11-13): Kokoro on MLX starts English speech in 185 ms at the median; 30 of 35 audio checks passed. docs/features/voice-stays-local.md

  • Whole setups (2026-11-13, Soniox v5, one run each): bench/results/combinations.md. Its counts treat a skipped check as passed.

Not measured for any component here: a recorded person.

Cost arithmetic

A busy hour on the best setup: brain 60 replies at $0.0045, about $0.27. Mouth 60 replies of 30 words, about 11,000 characters at $0.04 per 1,000, about $0.43. Reader 10 reports at $0.01, about $0.10. Soniox $0.12 per stream hour with up to two streams, about $0.24. About $1 in all. Prices read 2026-11-06; check them. This is arithmetic, not a metered bill.

Other setups, the same hour, from the measured cost per reply in bench/results/brain_substance.md and the mouth table above:

The gate adds a small amount on every setup. Two runs disagree on what gpt-6-sol costs a reply: $0.0027 (bench/results/brain_substance.md) and $0.0194 (the 2026-11-25 brain run above); the two scripts count cache writes and thinking tokens differently, so the OpenAI row shows both.

Where the ears prices come from, read on the vendors' pages on 2026-11-19: Soniox real-time transcription $0.12 an hour per stream (soniox.com/pricing); gemini-3.5-transcribe-live $0.005 a minute of audio in and $0.004 a minute of text out, so $0.30 to $0.54 an hour (Google's pricing page); gpt-4o-transcribe about $0.006 a minute (OpenAI's pricing page). The brain and reader prices behind the per-reply costs (read 2026-11-06) were the same on those pages on 2026-11-19: Claude Sonnet 5.5 $2 and $10 per million tokens in and out (Anthropic), gemini-3.8-flash $0.75 and $3.75, gpt-6-sol $2 and $10. Google's page says gemini-3.8-flash and gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts double on 2027-01-01: the two paid Gemini-only rows then rise by about $0.40 and $0.30.
