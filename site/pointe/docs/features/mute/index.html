Mute

One button mutes Pointe's voice. Everything it would say arrives as text in the conversation, and you read it. You still talk or type to it, and approvals work as they always do. For a quiet room, a call you are on, or anyone who reads faster than they listen. It replaces reading mode, and keeps its two phrases.

The rules live in pointe/mute.py (the phrases, the words, the status); the daemon applies them in one place, daemon.MuteOut, between the brain and the mouth.

Use it

  • The button: a speaker, beside the call's status line, on the page, in the iPhone app and in the Mac app. The icon shows the state now: sound waves while Pointe has a voice; muted, a slash and an orange ring. Its label says what a tap does: "Mute Pointe", or "Unmute Pointe".

  • The status line: while Pointe speaks it says "Speaking…". Muted, it says "Writing…" whenever Pointe would have spoken, and the reply is in the conversation.

  • Voice: "stop talking, I'll read", "I'll read", "reading mode", "text only", "מצב קריאה" mute it. "Talk again", "you can talk again", "reading mode off", "back to normal", "תדבר שוב", "חזרה לרגיל" unmute it, only while it is muted. Typed, the same words do the same. Each must be a whole short utterance; the voice model never gets them. Pointe answers in text: "Muted: I'll write instead of talking. Say "talk again" to hear me.", and "Talking again." aloud when it ends.

Muting in the middle of a reply stops the audio at once, on the Mac and on the device you hear it from. The reply goes on as text: the sentences Pointe had not said yet are written.

Each device keeps its own choice. The page keeps it in the browser, each app on its device. A device says its choice when it calls (?reading=1 on the call's address), so the first greeting is already text, and a call that reconnects is still muted. Muted or unmuted by voice during a call, the choice is sent to the device on the call, which keeps it. While a call is up every screen shows the call's choice, and the button on any of them changes it. With no call up, the button changes only that device's choice.

AirPods and play/pause (Mac app)

Mac app only, and only during a call:

  • An AirPods stem press turns the microphone off and on. It is macOS's own mute gesture, with the system's chime, and the status line says "Mic off". In push to talk the press talks instead, as on the iPhone. The window shows "AirPods: press for mic off" (or "for mic on", or "to talk") beside the talk mode; the bubble shows an AirPods glyph with the same words on hover.

  • Play/pause mutes Pointe. This covers another headset's button, the keyboard's ⏯ key, and an AirPods press that macOS sends as play/pause. Settings → This Mac → Play/pause in a call chooses Mute Pointe (the default), Skip, or Nothing.

  • Outside a call Pointe takes neither, so play/pause and the media keys stay with your music. During a call Pointe's call is the Now Playing item in Control Center. With Nothing, play/pause is never taken.

  • One press does one thing. If macOS sends the same press both ways, the first one counts and the other is dropped for half a second.

Why it is built this way, with Apple's documentation: design/macos-app.md, "The AirPods press".

What it does

  • Nothing is synthesised. A line for the voice never reaches the mouth, so no text-to-speech request is made and no vendor bills for one. The "One sec." filler is not shown either.

  • The text arrives as it always did: each line in the conversation, as on any call.

  • "Writing…" shows for two seconds after a line was written instead of spoken (mute.WRITING_SECS).
  • Chimes stay. A chime is not speech: a held approval in session mode still gets its one short cue.

Approvals and read-backs: unchanged

  • A permission request is shown word for word, as its read-out. A tap answers it (Face ID or Touch ID in the apps), or a spoken yes or no under the usual rules. Muted, the bot's turn starts and ends the moment the line is shown, so a yes counts only when begun after the line appeared and with nothing said between: the same rule as after the voice stops.

  • A read-back (a new session, sessions to add, a night's plan, the meeting's action items, a close) is shown as text. Its card's button confirms it, or a spoken yes under the read-back's rules.

  • A typed yes confirms nothing, as without mute. In the apps a tap asks for Face ID or Touch ID; a typed "yes" must not get around that. tests/test_mute.py runs the same approval answers unmuted and muted and asserts the same outcomes, and checks that a typed yes approves nothing.

With other modes

  • Drive mode holds it. On the road you cannot read, so Pointe talks while you drive; mute applies again when you stop. Asked for while driving, it waits: "Mute starts when drive mode ends. I keep talking while you drive." The button says "Drive mode holds mute: Pointe talks while you drive."

  • Whisper mode: both apply. Nothing is spoken, so the halved volume does not matter; the raised microphone and the short replies still do.

  • Push to talk and the microphone's own switch: unchanged; they are about what Pointe hears. With the microphone off the status line says "Mic off".

  • Meeting mode: what Pointe would say into the room (participating) is shown instead.

  • "Back to normal" ends one mode per utterance: drive mode, then a challenge, then whisper mode, then mute.

What went with reading mode

The Read button in the page's dock, the Read switch in the apps' Settings, the card ("Reading mode: Pointe writes, you read", Talk again, Mark all read) and the Unread and Read marks under each reply are gone. Nothing marks a reply read. A device that had reading mode on is muted.

Limits

  • Only on the default voice pipeline (the brain). With a realtime front (CLAUDE_VOICE_FRONT=gemini|openai) the model makes its own audio, and mute does not stop it.

  • A close by voice ("close billing and pricing") has no card: confirm it with a spoken yes, or close each session from its row.

  • An iPhone or Mac app from before 0.8.3 on a call with a 0.8.3 Mac still shows its Read switch and card; they work.

API

The names are reading mode's, so an app built before the mute button still works on a call.
POST /voice/reading {on: true\|false} {reading, applied}. Sets the call's choice; applied: false with no call up. 400 unless on is a boolean
call address /voice/call?reading=1, /voice/phone?reading=1: the calling device's choice
daemon to client, call socket {"type": "reading", "on": true\|false} when the choice changed by voice
status reading {on, held, writing, read_through}. held is "drive" while drive mode suspends it; writing is true for a moment after a line was shown instead of spoken
POST /voice/read, status talk[].unread, read_through what an older app's Unread and Read marks use; nothing in 0.8.3 shows them
