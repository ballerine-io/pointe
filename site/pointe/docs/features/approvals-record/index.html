Approvals record

Every answer to a permission request that went through Pointe is kept, and you can read it: when it was answered, whether by voice or by a tap, the exact request, and for a spoken answer the voice lock's score of the voice that said it. A spoken "yes" leaves no click behind; this is where it leaves a line.

The rules live in pointe/approvals.py; the daemon writes one line per answer (daemon._apply_answer) and serves the record at GET /voice/approvals, behind the same lock as every other route.

Use it

Surface Where
The page Settings → Approvals record
iPhone Settings (•••) → Approvals record
Mac Settings → Approvals record

It is read only: nothing in it answers a request. There is no phrase for it; ask Pointe "where is the approvals record" and it tells you the path.

What a row says

Part Example Notes
The answer and how Approved by voice · Denied by a tap · Approved from a notification "by a tap" is the card on the page, the iPhone or the Mac; "from a notification" is the action on an alert (Alerts)
When 20 Nov, 09:14 the Mac's own clock
The session bluebell/dev · devbox with its machine when it is not this Mac
The request Bash then git push --force-with-lease origin retry-fix word for word what the card showed: the tool, then its input. Secret-looking values are masked, as everywhere in the journal. Over 4,000 characters it is cut, and the row says so
The voice lock Voice lock approvals: score 0.71 only for a spoken answer. "The voice was not scored" when the lock could not tell (too short, no voiceprint for this device); "Voice lock off" when the lock was off. A tap has no voice, so no line

The score is the one the voice lock took its decision on: the likeness of the voice to your voiceprint, from 0 to 1. A spoken yes that the lock refused approved nothing, so it is not in the record.

What it holds, and for how long

  • An answer that reached the session: approved or denied, by voice, by a tap, by a notification's action. An answer that did not arrive ("the answer did not reach billing") is not a row.

  • Not an answer given in the session's own terminal: that never went through Pointe.

  • The record is the daemon's journal (~/.claude-voice/journal.jsonl and the one older file it rotates to at 20 MB), read back each time you open it. Nothing else is stored. It is as old as the journal is long, it shows the newest 200, and deleting the journal empties it. The journal never leaves the Mac; the iPhone reads the record over the paired link.

  • A request answered before this version has no row: the journal then held only the session's name and the answer.

What Where
