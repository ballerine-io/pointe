Brain dump

Dump your thoughts; Pointe stays quiet and keeps every word. Say done, and it structures them into a prompt in your own words (goal, context, constraints, open questions) for you to review. Only when you say send does it go anywhere: to the session you were pinned to, or to the orchestrator, as you were talking when you started. This replaces "brainstorm"; every old phrase still works.

The rules live in pointe/braindump.py (what starts and ends it, the review commands, the record, structuring and its guard, the file); pointe/daemon.py runs it. Design and the bugs it fixed: docs/design/brain-dump.md.

Use it

  • Voice: every step below has its phrase, so a whole dump works by voice alone.
  • Page: Brain dump in the dock. While you dump it reads Done; in review it reads Review.
  • iPhone app: the same button in the bottom bar, with the same three words.
  • Mac app: the same button in the bottom bar; Call → Brain Dump (⌘B) in the menu bar; or Brain Dump in the menu bar item's menu during a call.

The steps:

  • Start: "brain dump", "let me dump my thoughts", "just listen", "let me think out loud", "רק תקשיב"; or the Brain dump button (the page's dock, the app's bottom bar). A falling chime; the panel opens. "Brain dump." run straight into the first thought starts it too, with that thought kept.

  • Dump: talk. Every thought lands on the panel as it is heard, with the live line under it. Pauses of any length are fine. Pointe says nothing, session news waits, a permission request is not read out (a soft tone; its card shows).

  • Done: "done", "over to you", "what do you think", "structure it", "סיימתי"; or Done. Nothing said: back to the conversation, silently. Otherwise a rising chime, and in a few seconds the review. "Discard", "forget it", "never mind" (the whole utterance), or Discard, ends it with nothing kept.

  • Review: the structured prompt, editable, as plain sections (Goal, Context, Constraints, Open questions; empty ones left out) with bullets; a To: field (Orchestrator first, then the sessions; preselected where you were when the dump started: the pinned session in session mode, the orchestrator in orchestrator mode); Send (two clicks: the first reads "Tap again to send"), Add more (keep dumping; the next Done folds the new thoughts in), Read it back, Challenge it (devil's advocate on the prompt), Discard (two clicks). By voice: "send it" (the preselected target), "send it to X", "send it to the orchestrator" ("תשלח את זה לאורקסטרטור"), "add more", "read it back", "discard". Anything else you say is ordinary conversation; the card waits.

  • Send to a session: the text as shown goes to the session word for word, headed [from the user, by voice: their brain dump for X, in their own words, structured; nothing added]. Pointe says it went. It shows in Recent requests like a spoken send.

  • Send to the orchestrator: the text as shown becomes your turn to the voice model ("My brain dump, structured:" above it), the same path as words typed in the app. It answers, or sends it on to a session the way it handles any request, with every check send_to_claude_code makes. In session mode that one turn gets orchestrator routing: it may go to any session. The mode does not change; the next thing you say is pinned to the session again.

  • The pinned session is gone by the review (it left the voice): the To: field falls back to the orchestrator and says "X is gone, so this goes to the orchestrator." A session that was renamed, or came back under the same name, is still the one.

What holds, in code

  • Nothing said while dumping reaches the voice model or a session. Done tells the model one line ("ready to review"); the thoughts themselves never go to it unless you say "read it back".

  • The prompt is your words: an item whose words are not mostly yours is dropped (braindump.invented, tested); when the model fails or has no key, the prompt is your thoughts under Context, still editable and sendable (the review says so). Credentials in a thought are masked as everywhere else.

  • A spoken "send it to X" while still dumping structures and preselects X; the send waits for "send" or the button, so what goes is what was shown.

  • The send still refuses what a spoken send refuses by rule: during a meeting (409), a relay of an approval the session takes only typed (409), and a prompt touching production credentials once, with the risk stated (a second Send goes).

  • A yes said while dumping approves nothing (the pending request is asked again at the review). Session news held during the dump is told after the review line.

  • The dump is written to ~/.claude-voice/brain_dump.json on every change. A dropped call, a locked phone, a closed tab or a daemon restart loses nothing: on the next call the panel shows it and Pointe says it is still here (keep going, say done, or discard).

  • A meeting that starts takes the thoughts as its brief; "challenge this" takes them as the plan. Both end the dump.

  • Not in a meeting (409). Works in drive mode by voice alone.
  • Where it started is kept in the dump (route: the mode, and the pinned session by id and name) and on disk, so a restart does not change the default. A dump saved by an older version has none and follows the routing now.

API
POST /voice/dump {action: start \| done \| more \| edit \| discard \| send \| read, text?, session?, to?} → {brain_dump, sent_to?}. Send: session names one, to: "orchestrator" the orchestrator, neither goes to send_to. 400 on a bad action or an edit without text; 409 with the reason: no call, a meeting on, a dump already in review, nothing to send, no such session, offline, credentials (once). Page cookie, bearer token, phone key
POST /voice/listen {on}: the old switch; on = start, off = done
status brain_dump null, or {id, state: dumping \| structuring \| review, since, thoughts: [{text, at}], dropped, prompt: {goal, context, constraints, open_questions, text} \| null, target, problem, resumed, fallback, route, send_to: {orchestrator, session, gone}}
status monologue while dumping, {since, said}: the old field, for an older app
status mode_state.mode brain-dump while dumping
