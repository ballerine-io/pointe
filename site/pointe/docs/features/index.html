Features

How each feature behaves now. The daemon holds the state and enforces the rules, so the page, the iPhone app and the Mac app never disagree. Most features work in all four ways in: by voice, on the page, in the iPhone app and in the Mac app. The second table says where one does not, and each page says why.

Two rules hold for all of them: a mode or feature can only narrow what a spoken word may do, never widen it; and a no from any voice denies a pending request, in every mode.

Feature In one line
Voice lock Only your voice counts, for approvals or for everything; checked on this Mac
Self-learning vocabulary Fix a misheard name once, and the next call hears it right
Drive & walk mode Short answers, nothing to look at, and only safe requests approved by voice
Push to talk Nothing leaves the Mac unless you hold the key or the button
Audio briefings "Brief me on the night": what each session did, failed and waits on
Brain dump Dump your thoughts; Pointe structures them into a prompt in your words, and sends it only when you say
Challenge me The voice argues against your plan, grounded in your sessions, and cannot send
Wrist approvals and desktop alerts A session needs you: the watch taps, the desktop notifies
Show while it says The file, diff or failing test appears on screen as the voice mentions it
Whisper mode Quiet replies, a raised microphone, the same approval rules
Mute One button beside the status line: Pointe writes every reply instead of speaking, approvals unchanged
Skip Skip what Pointe is saying and go on to the next message: a button, a key on the Mac, or "skip"; a switch drops what waited
Thinking and the waiting sound From the end of your turn to the reply the status says "Thinking…", and one soft sound marks a long wait
Progress updates Off by default: one short true line while a session works, 20 seconds in and then every 45
Approvals record Each answer that went through Pointe: when, by voice or by a tap, the exact request, the voice lock's score
Accessibility A name for every control, nothing told by sound, motion or colour alone, Reduce Motion, large text
Conflict alarm Two sessions claim opposite things about the same work: you hear it at once
Meeting mode Notes, lookups and a raised hand in a meeting, with consent and no sends
Start a new session "Start a Codex session in shop": read back, started on your yes, only in folders you already work in
Add sessions "Connect to the session about the pentest", "reopen yesterday's session about billing": found, read back, joined on your yes; no typing in the session
Night shift "Tonight: billing fixes the retry tests": the sessions work while you sleep, nothing is approved for you, and the morning briefing leads with it
Clean up my words Under each thing you say, a clean version of what the voice understood
Voice stays on your Mac Ears and mouth on your Mac, no audio to any vendor; the cloud brain gets text only
Fully local Every model on your Mac, no keys, nothing sent out; slower and less accurate, by measure
OpenAI models from another host Azure OpenAI or another OpenAI-compatible host for the text models, with the key in the Keychain; not measured

Core

Always on, and described elsewhere:

  • Several sessions, one voice. Say a session's name to talk to it, ask "by the way" questions, choose Orchestrator or Session: What it does, and the targeting rule in How it works. Session mode speaks only about the session in focus; the others' news waits as a count on the screen: Session mode.

  • Approvals. A spoken yes approves only right after the daemon's own read-out; a tap always works: Approvals, exactly.

  • The live transcript, typing and correcting. The conversation as it happens, Type instead of talking (Enter sends; Shift+Enter, Option+Enter on the Mac, or New line on the iPhone starts a new line, and the line breaks reach the session), and a fix to what was heard: What it does; what a fix teaches is in Self-learning vocabulary.

  • Turn taking. "Don't interrupt me" holds the voice until "go ahead"; Settings → Wait before answering sets how long a pause ends your sentence: design/turn-taking.md.

  • Machines. Sessions on your other Macs and Linux machines on the same voice: Machines.

  • Harnesses. Claude Code, OpenCode and Codex, and how to add another: Harnesses.

The rest of the core, which these build on:

  • How it works: the pipeline, the rules enforced in code, echo handling.
  • Connect your phone: routes and pairing; The iPhone app: building it, alerts and the Apple Watch.
  • The Mac app: the menu bar item, the window, the menus and their keys.
  • Security: the threat model and what each route and key can do.
