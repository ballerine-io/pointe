Conflict alarm

One session reports "the migration passed"; twelve minutes later another reports "the test using the new column failed". Pointe says so at once, with both claims, and offers to put a question to either session. It never asks a session on its own.

It works for sessions of every harness. The rules live in pointe/conflicts.py; the daemon starts a check after each session report and delivers what it accepts.

Use it

  • The voice says it, and a card shows the two claims with Ask (either side) and Dismiss. One card at a time, the newest, with "N more disagreements" under it. The app plays a warning haptic when a card appears.

  • Ask sends the card's question, as the card shows it, straight to that session, under a header saying it is a conflict check written by Pointe, not typed by you. It never becomes your words: it starts no brain turn, passes no send check as if you had said it, and approves nothing.

  • Dismiss (a tap, or "I know" / "later" within 5 minutes of the spoken alarm) keeps that pair quiet for the rest of the call.

  • Settings: conflicts.on (default on; off means no check at all) and conflicts.spoken (default on; off is "Card only").

Where:

  • Voice: the alarm is spoken unless the setting is Card only; "I know" or "later" dismisses it, as above. No phrase turns the check on or off.

  • Page: the card under the conversation; Settings → Conflict alarm → "Check sessions against each other" and Alarm (Spoken or Card only).

  • iPhone app: the same card, with the haptic; Settings (••• at the top) → Conflict alarm, with the same two settings.

  • Mac app: the same card; Settings (••• at the top, or ⌘,) → Conflict alarm.

What is checked, in code

  • Pairs. Another registered session whose last report is under 4 hours old, and that shares a git root or folder, or shares two or more names and edited files with this one, or is named in its new message. Only edits count as touching a file; reads never do, and common names (index, main, README, …) are ignored.

  • Accepted only if the reading model says the claims conflict and are still current; each claim's outcome words and numbers appear in its own session's message (and at least half its content words); and the two claims do not say the same thing.

  • Limits. One alarm per pair per 30 minutes, the same claims never twice. Sessions on different branches are never urgent.

  • Speech. An urgent conflict jumps the queue; a normal one waits its turn. It waits while a push-to-talk press is open and while a read-out's yes window is open, so it never takes a yes away from a request or talks over you. It never touches a permission. During a meeting it is never spoken, not even after: the card and the note stay.

  • Secrets. The reading model's input and every field of an accepted conflict pass the credential mask, so status, notes, the phone and the voice never carry a session's credential.

  • Reading model down: no alarm, and a journal line conflict check skipped: ....

Alerts

Each open conflict is an attention item titled "Two sessions disagree", with the two session names only (the claims quote the sessions' messages and stay in the app and the page). It reaches iPhone notifications, mirrored to the watch with no actions, and the page's desktop alerts. See alerts.

Limits

Offline tests cover mixed harnesses on each adapter's real record format. There is no live end-to-end run with real OpenCode or Codex sessions, and no measured precision.

API

POST /voice/conflicts (page cookie, bearer token, phone key):

Body Does Errors
{action: "dismiss", id} closes the card and its note; the pair stays quiet for the call 404 unknown or closed id
{action: "ask", id, side: "a"\|"b"} sends the question to that session as a conflict check 400 bad side; 404; 409 no call, a meeting on, or the session left; 502 not delivered

Status conflicts: [{id, a, b, agent_a, agent_b, claim_a, claim_b, at_a, at_b, at, why, severity: high|normal, questions: {a, b}, branches: {a, b}|null, asked, state}], open ones only; a is the earlier report. Notes get kind conflict. Prefs conflicts.on, conflicts.spoken in GET/POST /voice/prefs.
