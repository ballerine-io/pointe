Pointe documentation

Pointe is a conversational layer for your coding agents. You talk to all your coding-agent sessions by voice: ask what each one did, switch by saying its name, and answer permission prompts out loud, under rules enforced in code. The practice is conversational engineering; "convo coding", for short. Pointe works with Claude Code, OpenCode and Codex, and runs on your Mac with your own model keys, or with models on the Mac.
