Connect over Wi-Fi

The phone reaches the Mac's address on the network they share. You install nothing. It works only while both devices stay on that network, and until the Mac's address changes. For a route that works away from home, use Tailscale.

Two networks work:

  • The same Wi-Fi. The Mac and the phone are on one Wi-Fi network, at home or in an office that lets devices reach each other.

  • The iPhone's hotspot. Turn on Personal Hotspot on the iPhone and join it from the Mac. The phone then reaches the Mac on the hotspot's network, with no other Wi-Fi around.

Set it up

  1. Put the Mac and the phone on the same network (one of the two above).

  2. Pair. On the Mac, open Pointe's page: Settings → Phone → Connect your phone. Choose Same Wi-Fi (it shows the Mac's address), then Show the code. In a terminal instead: pointe pair wifi, then pointe restart.

  3. On the iPhone, scan the code in the Pointe app and confirm the Mac it names. (The Camera does not pair: a link it opens only takes you to the app's scanner.)

  4. The first time, iOS asks whether Pointe may find and connect to devices on your local network. Allow it: the Wi-Fi route needs it.

For the phone's browser instead of the app, run pointe pair link after step 2 and open the address once on the phone (In the phone's browser).

How it works

pointe pair wifi reads the Mac's address on en0 (or en1), makes a certificate for that address, and listens on it, port 7443 (pointe/pair.py). The app trusts that one certificate, by its fingerprint. The listener is on the Wi-Fi address only, not on every interface.

When it stops working

What you see Why Fix
"Not on your Mac's Wi-Fi", after the phone or the Mac moved One of them left the network Join the same network again
"Your Mac did not answer" on the same network The Mac's address changed (the router gave it another one, or you joined another network) Pair again: Connect your phone, Same Wi-Fi
The app cannot reach the Mac, while the phone's browser can The app's Local Network access is off (you said no when iOS asked) iPhone: Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, turn Pointe on
It works at home and not at the office Some networks keep devices from reaching each other (client isolation) Use the iPhone's hotspot, or Tailscale
The page says "No Wi-Fi address found." The Mac is not on Wi-Fi Join a Wi-Fi network, or the iPhone's hotspot

Pairing again on a new address makes a new certificate, so every phone and machine paired before must pair again.
