Connect over Tailscale

Tailscale puts the Mac and the phone on one private network, wherever each has internet. Pointe listens on the Mac's Tailscale address only, so nothing is open on the Wi-Fi or the internet. This is the route the page recommends when it finds Tailscale on the Mac.

Set it up

  1. Install Tailscale on the Mac and on the iPhone, and sign in to both with the same account.

  2. Pair. On the Mac, open Pointe's page: Settings → Phone → Connect your phone. Choose Tailscale (it shows the Mac's Tailscale name), then Show the code. In a terminal instead: pointe pair tailscale, then pointe restart.

  3. On the iPhone, scan the code in the Pointe app and confirm the Mac it names. (The Camera does not pair: a link it opens only takes you to the app's scanner.)

For the phone's browser instead of the app, run pointe pair link after step 2 and open the address once on the phone (In the phone's browser).

How it works

pointe pair tailscale asks the Tailscale app for this Mac's name (its MagicDNS name) and its IPv4 address (tailscale status --json), makes a certificate for that name, and listens on the Tailscale address, port 7443 (pair.py). The phone connects by the name; the app trusts that one certificate, by its fingerprint. Tailscale needs no account with Pointe and no server of yours.

When it stops working

What you see Why Fix
"Tailscale is not running on this Mac. Install it (https://tailscale.com/download), sign in, and retry." The Tailscale app is not installed, not running, or signed out Open Tailscale on the Mac and sign in
"Tailscale has no name or address for this Mac yet." Tailscale is still connecting Wait until it shows Connected, then pair again
"Mac not reachable" on the phone The phone's Tailscale is off, or signed in to another account Open Tailscale on the iPhone, turn it on, check the account
"Mac not reachable" while both are on Tailscale The Mac sleeps, or the phone listener is off Wake the Mac; on the page, Settings → Phone, check that Tailscale is "in use"
The phone reaches other Tailscale devices, not the Mac A Tailscale access rule blocks port 7443 to the Mac Allow the phone to reach the Mac on TCP 7443 in your tailnet's access controls

The Tailscale name stays the same when the Mac changes networks, so you pair once.
