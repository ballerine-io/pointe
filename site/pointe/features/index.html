Features

Everything Pointe does.

Sixty features in eight groups. Each name opens the page that explains it: how to use it, its rules and its limits.

You can use Pointe in three places: a page in your browser, the iPhone app and the Mac app. Most features work in all three. When one does not, the line says where it works. Why: the parity matrix. Version 1.20.0.

Talk

  • One conversation, every sessionAsk what each did. Background ones speak only when done or stuck
  • Send by nameSay a session's name, and your words go to it
  • "By the way" questionsAnswered from a read-only copy of the session
  • Push to talkHold or tap. No audio leaves the Mac unless you do
  • Talk without the screenA key, a shortcut from any app, or the Action button
  • Turn takingAnswers Quick, Normal or Patient; "don't interrupt me"
  • Type insteadTyped words are sent like speech, and never approve
  • Self-learning dictionaryFix a misheard name once; the next call hears it right
  • Clean up my wordsA clean version of what it understood, under your words
  • Show while it saysThe file, diff or failing test, as the voice mentions it
  • Whisper modeShort, quiet replies, under the same approval rules
  • English and HebrewBoth benchmarked. Hebrew lines read right to left

Sessions and machines

  • Claude Code, OpenCode, CodexSide by side in one call, each with its own approvals
  • Sessions by machineAgent and state of each. Rename, disconnect, filter
  • Orchestrator or SessionThe voice tracks them all, or everything goes to one
  • Session notesEach session's goal, state, decisions and open points
  • Recent requestsWhere each request went, or why the gate blocked it
  • Conflict alarmTwo sessions claim opposite things: you hear it at once
  • MachinesOnline, sessions, round trip, key. Invite or revoke one

Approvals and safety

  • A spoken yesOnly for a request the daemon just read out itself
  • A tap on the exact requestFace ID on the iPhone, Touch ID on the Mac
  • A no from anyone deniesIn every mode
  • The gateA model checks each send against what you just said
  • Voice lockOnly your voice counts. Checked on this Mac
  • Secrets maskedA credential-like value is not read out or shown
  • Deny and Approve on a notificationApprove is off by default, and only for a readable requestiPhone and Mac apps
  • Terminal-only approvalsOne setting keeps every prompt in the terminal

Thinking tools

  • Brain dumpThink aloud; get a prompt in your words, sent on your say
  • Challenge meIt argues against your plan, and cannot send
  • Meeting modeNotes, lookups and a raised hand. No sends
  • Audio briefings"Brief me on the night", on request or on a schedule

Privacy and local modes

  • No server, no account, no telemetryWhat leaves the Mac goes to vendors you chose
  • Your own keysIn the macOS Keychain; they reach the daemon only
  • A model for each roleEars, brain, reader, gate and mouth, set separately
  • Voice stays on your MacLocal ears and mouth: no audio to any vendor
  • Fully localEvery model on your Mac. Slower, less accurate, measured
  • Kept on the MacVoiceprint, call journal, session notes, dictionary
